ECONOMY GUIDE

WHEN TO BUY?

Managing your credits is the key to winning rounds.

  • Full Buy: Buy Vandal/Phantom + Full Shields when you have 3900+ credits.
  • Eco Round: Save your money! Buy nothing or just a cheap pistol if you are low.
  • Force Buy: Buy the best you can (Spectre/Ares) even if you can't afford a full buy.

MIN NEXT ROUND

The most important part of the shop menu.

  • Yellow Exclamation (!): Look for this in the top left of the shop.
  • Min Next Round: Shows exactly how much money you will have next round.
  • The Rule: Make sure this number is at least 3900 if you want to full buy next.

CREDIT REWARDS

How you earn your money each round.

  • Win: +3000 Credits.
  • Loss: +1900 / +2400 / +2900 (Loss streak bonus).
  • Kills: +200 Credits per kill.
  • Planting: +300 Credits for the whole team.

AGENTS

DUELISTS

The entry fraggers. They go in first and get kills.

  • Jett: Fast, dashes, updrafts. Best for OPing.
  • Raze: Explosives, grenades, satchels. High damage.
  • Reyna: Heals or dismisses after kills. Self-sufficient.
  • Phoenix: Flashes, fire walls, self-revive ult.
  • Neon: Super speed, slides, electric beam.
  • Iso: Bulletproof shield, 1v1 arena ult.
  • Yoru: Teleports, clones, invisibility. Tricky.
  • Waylay: Light speed dashes, invulnerability beacon.

INITIATORS

They gather info and set up the team for fights.

  • Sova: Recon dart, drone, shock darts. Wallhacks.
  • Fade: Reveals enemies, blinds, holds them in place.
  • Skye: Flashes, dog scout, heals team.
  • KAY/O: Suppresses abilities, flashes. Anti-ability.
  • Breach: Stuns, flashes through walls. Earthquakes.
  • Gekko: Little buddies plant spike and flash.
  • Tejo: Stealth drone, guided missiles, tactical strike.

CONTROLLERS

They smoke off angles and control the map.

  • Omen: Teleports, regenerating smokes, blind.
  • Brimstone: iPad smokes, molly, orbital strike.
  • Viper: Toxic walls, poison cloud, decay pit.
  • Astra: Stars, gravity well, cosmic divide. Big brain.
  • Harbor: Water walls, shield bubble.
  • Clove: Smokes even after death, self-revive.
  • Miks: Audio smokes, heal and concuss, stim. Ultimate is a deafening sonic wave.

SENTINELS

They lock down sites and watch flanks.

  • Killjoy: Turret, alarmbot, nanoswarms. Site anchor.
  • Cypher: Camera, tripwires, cages. Info king.
  • Sage: Wall, slow orbs, heal, revive. Support.
  • Chamber: Teleport, trap, big guns. Aggressive.
  • Deadlock: Sound sensors, barrier mesh, cocoon.
  • Vyse: Metal thorns, flash wall.
  • Veto: Utility destroyer, snare traps, mutated ultimate.

MAPS & BEST COMPS

ASCENT

Standard 2 sites. Mid control is key.

Best Agents: Sova, KAY/O, Killjoy, Jett, Omen.

BIND

No mid, teleporters. Fast rotations.

Best Agents: Raze, Skye, Brimstone, Viper, Cypher.

HAVEN

3 Sites (A, B, C). Lots of map to cover.

Best Agents: Jett, Breach, Skye, Omen, Killjoy.

SPLIT

High ground is important. Ropes.

Best Agents: Raze, Jett, Sage, Omen, Skye.

ICEBOX

Verticality, ziplines. Plant on B is hard.

Best Agents: Jett, Sova, Viper, Sage, Killjoy.

BREEZE

Huge open map. Long range fights.

Best Agents: Jett, Viper, Sova, KAY/O, Chamber.

FRACTURE

Defenders spawn in middle. Attackers pinch.

Best Agents: Raze, Breach, Brimstone, Chamber, Fade.

PEARL

Underwater city. Long B main.

Best Agents: Jett, Fade, Astra, KAY/O, Killjoy.

LOTUS

3 Sites, rotating doors.

Best Agents: Jett, Raze, Omen, Skye, Killjoy.

SUNSET

Standard layout, strong mid.

Best Agents: Raze, Cypher, Omen, Fade, Gekko.

ABYSS

No walls on edges, you can fall off!

Best Agents: Jett, Omen, Sova, Iso, Clove.

WEAPONS TIER LIST

S-TIER (GOD TIER)

  • Vandal: One tap headshot at any range. The GOAT.
  • Phantom: No tracers, fast fire rate. Good for spamming.
  • Operator: The big sniper. One shot body shot. Expensive.
  • Bandit: NEW! One tap headshot vs light armor. 600 creds.

A-TIER (REALLY GOOD)

  • Spectre: Run and gun SMG. Good for eco rounds.
  • Judge: Auto shotgun. Sit in a corner and click.
  • Ghost: Best pistol. Quiet and deadly.
  • Sheriff: Pocket Guardian. One tap headshot close range.
  • Guardian: Semi-auto. High damage, good zoom.
  • Outlaw: Double barrel sniper. High damage, fast fire.

B-TIER (SITUATIONAL)

  • Odin: LMG goes brrr. Wallbang everything. Expensive.
  • Ares: Mini Odin. Good for early round wallbangs.
  • Bulldog: Burst fire rifle. Good for mid-range eco.
  • Marshal: Cheap sniper. One tap headshot, fast movement.
  • Stinger: Hyper fire rate SMG. Deadly at close range.
  • Bucky: Pump shotgun. High damage but slow fire rate.
  • Classic: Free pistol. Right click is surprisingly good.
  • Shorty: Pocket shotgun. Best for holding tight corners.
  • Frenzy: Full auto pistol. Great for aggressive ecos.
